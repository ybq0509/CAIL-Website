Céline Yunya Song

Head and Professor, EMIA; Professor, Social Science · Emerging Interdisciplinary Areas; Social Science

Personal websitenorth_eastyunyasong@ust.hk

Biography

Céline Yunya Song is Head and Professor of the Division of Emerging Interdisciplinary Areas and Professor in the Division of Social Science at HKUST. She is also Director of the Media Intelligence Research Center. She received her PhD in Media and Communication from City University of Hong Kong in 2012.

Her research interests include media intelligence, global communication, cyberpsychology and behavior, and AI in the social sciences and humanities.

Research Interests

  • Media intelligence
  • Global communication
  • Cyberpsychology and behavior
  • AI in the social sciences and humanities
