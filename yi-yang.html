Yi Yang

Associate Professor; Lee Heng Fellow · Information Systems, Business Statistics and Operations Management

Personal websitenorth_eastimyiyang@ust.hk

Biography

Yi Yang is an Associate Professor and Lee Heng Fellow in the Department of Information Systems, Business Statistics and Operations Management, School of Business and Management, at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. He joined the department in July 2017 and is Director of the Center for Business and Social Analytics.

He is an Associate Editor for Information Systems Research and INFORMS Journal on Computing, and an Associate Editor for the MIS Quarterly special issues “The Institutional Press in the Digital Age” and “Ethics, Regulation, and Policy”. He is also a Senior Area Chair for ACL Rolling Review. His departmental profile additionally lists his service as Associate Editor for Decision Sciences from 2026.

His research interests include natural language processing, large language models, machine learning, statistical inference, and their applications in finance and business. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Northwestern University in 2015 and his bachelor’s degree from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2009.

Research Interests

  • Natural language processing
  • Large language models
  • Machine learning and deep learning
  • Statistical inference and applications in finance and business
