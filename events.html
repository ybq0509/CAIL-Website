2026-06-12

Trustworthy Legal Reasoning Workshop at ICAIL 2026

Organised by Professor Sirui Han’s team at HKUST, the Trustworthy Legal Reasoning Workshop brought together legal and AI researchers at Singapore Management University. Twelve presentations across three sessions examined evaluation, professional practice, rights protection and accountability in legal AI.

2026-09-04

LexiHK and Guangdong Judicial AI Teams Explore Cross Border Legal Technology Cooperation

LexiHK and Guangzhou Data Group’s judicial AI team held an initial exchange on 4 September 2026 under the Guangdong–Hong Kong smart city cooperation framework. The meeting explored legal AI applications across jurisdictions and identified potential collaboration in dispute resolution, financial compliance, practical use cases and legal datasets.

2026-02-04

Yikang Li Explores Quantitative Trading in the AGI Era

Dr Yikang Li delivered a seminar at HKUST on 4 February exploring the transition from AI towards artificial general intelligence and its potential implications for quantitative finance and trading. The event connected emerging research with perspectives from real-world financial applications.

2027-02-08

LEXFLOW 2027 Call for Papers on Agentic Workflows for Human AI Legal Reasoning

LEXFLOW 2027 invites contributions on intelligent interfaces for human–AI legal reasoning. Taking place alongside ACM IUI 2027 in Helsinki, the workshop will explore how people can understand, supervise and steer legal workflows involving multiple AI agents, tools and evidence sources.

2026-09-22

Trusted Legal Intelligence in Practice: Researchers and Practitioners Discuss Legal AI at HKUST

The Academic and Practice Seminar on Legal Technology brought together researchers, lawyers, legal information providers and technology developers at HKUST Business School Central on 22 September 2026. Discussions examined how legal AI can support professional work through reliable sources, verifiable reasoning and clear responsibilities.

2026-09-06

LexiHK Highlighted in China Daily Commentary on AI and Hong Kong Legal Services

In a China Daily commentary published on 6 September 2026, Thomas So highlighted LexiHK and its planned trial by Hong Kong’s Department of Justice. The article examined how AI adoption, shared infrastructure and digital dispute resolution could strengthen Hong Kong’s legal services.

2025-04-13

LexiHK Wins Bronze Medal at the 50th International Exhibition of Inventions Geneva

LexiHK received a bronze medal at the 50th International Exhibition of Inventions Geneva in April 2025. The legal AI project was among the inventions contributing to HKUST’s record achievement of 38 awards across 37 participating teams.
