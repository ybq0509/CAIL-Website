Work with CAIL

The Center welcomes interdisciplinary exchange on artificial intelligence, law and governance. Topics include legal reasoning, model safety, evidence verification, privacy and compliance evaluation, and human–AI collaboration in professional settings.

Joint Research

Collaborate around shared research questions, combining technical methods, legal analysis and empirical research to develop benchmarks, research prototypes and reproducible findings.

Professional Exchange

Bring legal and regulatory challenges into research through seminars, workshops and case discussions, connecting researchers and practitioners on system capabilities, boundaries and responsibility.

Academic Exchange

Support research sharing, lectures and academic visits across disciplines. Specific visiting, student research and joint-training opportunities will be announced separately.

Education Collaboration

Explore courses and exchange that connect research with professional learning in AI ethics, generative AI governance, legal reasoning and responsible applications. Existing teaching includes Artificial Intelligence Ethics, Generative Artificial Intelligence Governance, Artificial Intelligence for Financial Economics, and Law and Economics.
