Zhesheng Qiu

Visiting Assistant Professor · Economics

Personal Homepagenorth_eastCVnorth_eastzheshqiu@ust.hk

Biography

My research studies business-cycle dynamics through firm profits, inflation transmission, and belief formation, emphasizing transparent mechanisms and empirically disciplined frameworks that are robust to model specification.

I am visiting the Department of Economics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology from 09/2024 to now. I am also on the job market.

Research Interests

  • Business-cycle dynamics
  • Firm profits and inflation transmission
  • Belief formation
  • Monetary policy and production networks
