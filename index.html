About CAIL

Morearrow_forward

The HKUST Center for Artificial Intelligence for Law brings together researchers in artificial intelligence, law, public policy, business and social science to study the reliability, governance and application of AI in legal and other high-stakes professional settings. Starting from practical problems in legal reasoning, evidence verification and professional decision-making, the Center connects technical research with institutional analysis.

2026-09-22

Legal Technology Academic and Practice Seminar

The Academic and Practice Seminar on Legal Technology, “Trusted Legal Intelligence in Practice,” was co-hosted by the AI & Law team of HKUST’s Division of Emerging Interdisciplinary Areas and the LexiHK team at HKUST Business School Central on 22 September 2026, combining on-site discussion with Zoom participation. Speakers from universities, legal information providers, legal-technology firms and law firms discussed verification, cross-jurisdictional differences and professional responsibility.

2026-06-12

ICAIL 2026 Trustworthy Legal Reasoning Workshop

Organised by Sirui Han’s team at HKUST, the Trustworthy Legal Reasoning Workshop at ICAIL 2026 brought together legal and AI researchers at Singapore Management University. Twelve papers across three sessions examined model evaluation, professional practice, rights protection and governance. Discussions highlighted human baselines, context-sensitive evaluation and mechanisms for review and correction.
