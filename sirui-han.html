Sirui Han

Director, CAIL · Assistant Professor, HKUST

Personal profilenorth_eastsiruihan@ust.hk

Biography

Sirui Han is an interdisciplinary scholar working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, law, finance, and social science. She is an Assistant Professor at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and Associate Director of the HKUST-DXM AI for Finance Joint Laboratory. Her research examines legal and financial applications of generative AI, trustworthy and safe large language models, computational approaches to law and policy, and the governance of emerging technologies. She holds a PhD in Laws from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and is completing a PhD in Computer Science at HKUST.

Research Interests

  • Artificial intelligence; large language models
  • AI governance and safety; LegalTech
  • Law and finance; jurisprudence and public law
  • Computational social science
